# Architected matter examples

Open these in **Panels > Matter** (`concepts.architected-matter`). The material values
are representative handbook values, labelled as such; process limits are illustrative.

| Project | What it shows |
|---|---|
| `a-homogeneous-plate.resonant` | a conventional material as an architecture: one region, one layer, its plate modes |
| `b-energy-converter.resonant` | a printed steel cantilever with a bonded PZT plate, electrodes and encapsulation (harvests, can be made), and the same function with a sol-gel film that delaminates on cooling from 923 K |
| `c-thermal-barrier.resonant` | a superalloy wall with a bond coat and a zirconia top coat, a contact resistance at the oxide: through-thickness conduction and the coupled engine |
| `d-graded-metal-ceramic.resonant` | nickel graded to alumina (an idealized profile, combined by a stated bound) against two discrete layers |
| `e-em-absorber.resonant` | a Salisbury screen: a 377 ohm per square film a quarter wave in front of a metal plate |
| `f-multimaterial-structure.resonant` | a frame with a lattice core (Gibson-Ashby), an embedded sensor and a channel: regions in three dimensions |
| `g-thickness-tradeoff.resonant` | an optimization problem: harvested power against the stiffness the load-bearing function needs (a Pareto set) |
| `h-oriented-film.resonant` | a drawn PVDF film along and across the beam: the coupling follows d31 or d32 |

Every example is run by `MatterExamples.*`.
