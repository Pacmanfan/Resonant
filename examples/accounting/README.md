# Accounting examples

Open these in **Panels > Accounting** (`concepts.accounting`). The values are illustrative.

| Project | What it shows |
|---|---|
| `a-closed-collision.resonant` | two gliders and a spring bumper: momentum conserved while kinetic energy turns elastic and back; each glider alone |
| `b-damped-driven-oscillator.resonant` | the work of a driving force stored or dissipated as heat; the residual at half the step |
| `c-resonator-buildup-decay.resonant` | Q = ω E / P by loss mechanism; a resonator driven for 20 s and ringing down |
| `d-coupled-resonators.resonant` | energy beating between two resonators: internal to the pair, a boundary flow of each |
| `e-microwave-absorption.resonant` | microwave power absorbed by a food layer and leaving as heat, through the coupled engine; heating step by step; an effective permittivity's scale contract |
| `f-radiation-pressure.resonant` | the radiation force on an absorber and a copper film by two methods |
| `g-material-energy.resonant` | plastic work, a ferrite core's hysteresis loss against its hysterons, a melting wall's energy and entropy |
| `h-torsional-flywheels.resonant` | angular momentum and torques of two flywheels on a shaft |

Every study is run by `AccountingExamples.*`.
