## Scope

Metamaterials: effective properties derived from structures (retrieval, layered anisotropy, thermal), resonant lattices, resonator ducts, split rings, effective materials in the library and the coupled engine

## Implementation

- Bloch analysis of lumped cells (`oscillators/BlochLattice`): mass and stiffness of a cell with phase-shifted links to the next, the Hermitian K(q), bands and gaps.
- Domains: `mechanics/LocallyResonantLattice` (mass-in-mass dispersion, gap, effective dynamic mass, finite chain); `manipulators/ResonatorDuct` (cell transfer matrices with shunt Helmholtz admittances, Bloch, gaps, effective bulk modulus, finite-array transmission); `electromagnetics/ObliqueLayers` (TE/TM stacks with losses, the uniaxial slab, oblique Bloch), `Retrieval` (impedance-and-index retrieval with branch continuity, passivity, conditioning, locality and antiresonance flags), `SplitRing` (circuit model, Lorentzian permeability, Kramers-Kronig residual); `thermal/LayeredConductivity` (series and parallel, checked by finite volumes).
- `functional/EffectiveMaterial` (the effective-material record and its library form) and the analysis category `effective-medium` (`functional/Analysis.{h,cpp}`).
- `coupling/MetamaterialAnalyses` (five analyses with representations, validity and effective materials), registered in `FunctionalAnalyses.cpp`.
- Material provenance `homogenized` (`materials/Material.{h,cpp}`, the materials dialog, the contract value 6 and its conversions).
- `FunctionalStructureService::createEffectiveMaterial` (`Services.h`, `FunctionalServices.cpp`), the RPC `CreateEffectiveMaterial` (`functional_service.proto`, `Handlers.cpp`), the MCP tool `create_effective_material`, the panel's Save effective material (`FunctionalPanel.{h,cpp}`, `forms/FunctionalPanel.ui`, `FunctionalController.{h,cpp}`).
- Examples `examples/metamaterials/a-mass-in-mass-lattice` ... `e-layered-thermal` (generated by `tmp/make_metamaterial_examples.py`, not committed) and their README.
