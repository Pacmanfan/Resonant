## Scope

Multiscale material modeling: representations across scales with lineage and model cards, voxel RVEs and computational homogenization, atomistic crystals and Lennard-Jones reference calculations, external engines as adapters, derivation pipeline, sequential and iterative coupling

## Implementation

- Multiscale model (`multiscale/Representation`): representations per scale with revisions, relationships, derivation records, storage in the project extension `multiscale`, staleness and lineage navigation; `ModelCards`, `Couplings` (contracts), `ExternalSolvers` (detection on PATH, none integrated); `Microstructure` (seeded voxel RVE generators, imported voxels); `PotentialHomogenization` (trilinear FE with periodic, linear, flux and mixed boundaries, bounds and the self-consistent estimate). Layering rules for `multiscale` and the `atomistic` domain (`tools/check_layering.py`).
- Atomistic domain (`atomistic/Crystal`, `StructureImport`, `ForceField`, `LatticeStatics`, `MolecularDynamics`): crystals and builders, XYZ and POSCAR import, Lennard-Jones force fields with applicability, lattice statics with tail corrections and elastic constants, NVE dynamics.
- Materials: provenances computed, database, fitted, surrogate; a derivation id and a standard uncertainty per value (`materials/Material`, `ProjectSerializer.cpp`, the materials dialog, the contract and its conversions).
- Coupling: `MultiscaleDerivations` (methods, applicability, RVE and atomistic derivations with size studies, uncertainty and reliability, assignment plans) and `MultiscaleCoupling` (local states from coupled results, iterative region-RVE coupling).
- `MultiscaleService` (17 operations; `Services.h`, `MultiscaleServices.cpp`, `ProjectServices.cpp`), capabilities (`ApplicationServices.cpp`), the contract `proto/resonant/v1/multiscale/*.proto`, handlers, MCP tools, the Material Scales dock (`MultiscaleController`, `MaterialScalesPanel`, `forms/MaterialScalesPanel.ui`, `MainWindow`).
- Examples `examples/multiscale/a-crystalline-argon` ... `e-cross-scale-investigation` (generated by `tmp/make_multiscale_examples.py`, not committed) and their README.
